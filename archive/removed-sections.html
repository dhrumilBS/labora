Can doctors sign reports digitally?

Yes. Pathologists, radiologists, and cardiologists review the report, preview it, and sign and send it in one step. Drafts save automatically while they work.

Does Labora support home sample collection?

Yes. Bookings are organized by status, phlebotomists are assigned to visits, and you can follow each visit on a live map with an estimated arrival time. Patients are kept informed by SMS.

Can Labora work with outsource or partner labs?

Yes. Tests sent to an outsource lab stay linked to the patient case, and business from partner labs (B2B) is tracked separately in your revenue and dues.

What business reports does Labora provide?

Labora shows billing, collections, and outstanding dues, revenue by day and by center, business from partner labs and home collection, and referring doctors ranked by cases and revenue, with week-over-week trends.

What is LIMS software?

LIMS stands for Laboratory Information Management System. LIMS software manages the lifecycle of samples and their data: registration, labeling, storage location, assigned tests, results, review, approval, and reporting, along with chain of custody and an audit trail of who did what and when. Learn about Labora LIMS.

What is the difference between LIMS and ELN?

A LIMS is structured around samples and repeatable testing processes, while an electronic lab notebook (ELN) is structured around experiments: protocols, observations, results, and attachments. Many labs need both. Labora connects them, so an experiment in the ELN can reference the exact samples, reagents, and instruments recorded in the LIMS.

Can laboratory management software track samples?

Yes. Sample tracking is one of the core jobs of laboratory management software. In Labora, each sample gets a unique ID and a record that follows it from registration through processing, testing, review, approval, reporting, and archiving, including its storage location and chain of custody.

Can Labora manage laboratory inventory?

Yes. Labora tracks reagents and consumables by lot, quantity, storage location, and expiration date. It can alert your team when stock runs low or items approach expiry, and link inventory usage to the experiments and tests that consumed it.

Can Labora automate laboratory workflows?

Yes. You can define workflows with triggers, steps, rules, and approvals, for example routing a sample to review automatically when testing is complete, or escalating an out-of-range result to a QA reviewer. Automation reduces repetitive handoffs and keeps work consistent.

Can Labora manage laboratory equipment?

Yes. Labora keeps a record of each instrument with its availability, bookings, maintenance and calibration schedule, usage, and service history, and alerts the right people before calibration or maintenance falls due.

Can Labora generate laboratory reports?

Yes. Labora generates reports from the data already captured in the platform, such as sample results, turnaround time, workload, inventory usage, and equipment utilization, and presents operational metrics in dashboards your team can filter and share.

Can Labora integrate with laboratory instruments?

Labora is designed to connect with instruments and other business systems through its integration layer and API. Supported connections depend on your instruments and setup, so the best next step is to review your equipment list with our team during a demo.

Is Labora suitable for growing laboratories?

Yes. Labora is a cloud platform built to scale with you, from a single lab to multiple sites, more users, and higher sample volumes, without rebuilding your processes each time you grow.

Can laboratory workflows be customized?

Yes. Workflows, sample types, fields, statuses, approval steps, and user roles can be configured to match how your lab actually works, rather than forcing your team into a fixed process.

How does Labora improve laboratory data management?

Labora captures laboratory data once and connects it across samples, experiments, inventory, and equipment. With structured records, permissions, and audit trails, your data stays consistent, searchable, and traceable, and reporting no longer means stitching spreadsheets together.
